# Sources

## Source Categories

### 1. Manual Seed Companies
Initial list of Irvine-based or Irvine-relevant companies curated manually.

### 2. Company Career Pages
Official company hiring pages. These are the highest quality sources.

### 3. Job Boards
Used as secondary discovery sources for job postings and company leads.

### 4. Local Directories
Used for discovering companies that may not appear prominently on major job boards.

## Source Priority

1. Official company career pages
2. Structured job boards
3. Local directories
4. Manual additions

## Initial Data Fields to Capture

### Company
- company_name
- website
- headquarters_location
- irvine_relevance
- industry
- company_size
- career_page_url
- notes

### Job Posting
- job_title
- company_name
- location
- remote_policy
- job_url
- posted_date
- employment_type
- tech_stack_tags
- seniority
- description_summary

## Risks

- job board scraping restrictions
- inconsistent page structures
- incomplete location data
- duplicate job postings across sources

## MVP Source Strategy

Start with:
- manually curated seed companies
- official company career pages
- 1–2 structured job board patterns
